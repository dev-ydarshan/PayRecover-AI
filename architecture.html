Architecture at a Glance

1 · DetectFailed transactions and customer context are read from Supabase.
2 · DecideThe seven-rule engine selects the cheapest appropriate recovery tier.
3 · ProtectStopping rules re-check payment state, fraud, attempts, and call windows.
4 · ExecuteAuto Retry, WhatsApp, or Voice Call runs through the DRY_RUN safety gate.
5 · AuditEvery decision, send, refusal, and recovery is appended to the audit trail.
6 · RecoverSuccessful payments update transaction status and credit the responsible tier.
